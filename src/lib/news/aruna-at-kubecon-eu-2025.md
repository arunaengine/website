## Aruna at KubeCon EU 2025: Unlocking Data and Workflow Orchestration

The Aruna team is excited to announce its participation at KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe 2025, taking place in London from April 1st to 4th. As an experienced player in the cloud-native data management space, the Aruna team  will be presenting cutting-edge innovations in Kubernetes-native data handling and workflow management.

### ArgoCon Talk: One Engine to Rule Them All

Aruna is also taking the stage at ArgoCon, a co-located event at KubeCon, with the talk "One Engine to Rule Them All: Unifying Cloud Workloads With Argo Workflows". This session explores a bold vision: What if Argo Workflows could be your universal scheduling engine? The talk will dive into how Argo Workflows can streamline cloud compute infrastructures, providing a single, unified interface for workflow automation across various platforms.

[**📅 Tuesday April 1, 2025 13:30 - 13:55 BST - Level 1 | Hall Entrance S10 | Room A**](https://colocatedeventseu2025.sched.com/event/1u5dO/one-engine-to-rule-them-all-unifying-cloud-workloads-with-argo-workflows-sebastian-beyvers-giessen-university)

### Poster Session: From Pods to Petabytes

In the Poster Session track, the Aruna team will showcase "From Pods to Petabytes: Managing Data Objects as Kubernetes Resources". This presentation delves into how Kubernetes’ powerful orchestration capabilities can be extended to manage data lifecycles in distributed environments. Attendees will gain insights into treating data as first-class citizens within Kubernetes, enabling scalable and efficient data operations across cloud-native applications.

[**📅 Thursday April 3, 2025 13:15 - 14:15 BST - Level 1 | Hall Entrances S8 - S9, N8 - N9**](https://kccnceu2025.sched.com/event/1txDl/poster-session-from-pods-to-petabytes-managing-data-objects-as-kubernetes-resources-sebastian-beyvers-jannis-hochmuth-giessen-university?iframe=yes&w=100%&sidebar=yes&bg=no)
