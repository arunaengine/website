## Aruna team hosts successful workshop at NFDI4Microbiota annual conference

On December 4th and 5th, 2024, the Aruna development team had the pleasure of participating in the NFDI4Microbiota annual conference, where they conducted an engaging and informative workshop. The session provided attendees with a comprehensive introduction to Aruna, its core concepts, and the latest developments in the platform.

The workshop began with an overview of Aruna’s fundamental principles, emphasizing its efficiency, scalability, and user-friendliness. The team then showcased the straightforward onboarding process, demonstrating how new users can quickly and easily get started. To further illustrate Aruna’s capabilities, the team led a hands-on tutorial covering the platform’s basic functionalities, ensuring that participants could experience its intuitive design firsthand.

One of the highlights of the workshop was the interactive Q&A session. Participants engaged in a lively discussion, sharing their feedback, experiences, and questions. The exchange of ideas reinforced the development team’s commitment to addressing user needs, and the positive reception of Aruna’s features confirmed that they are on the right track. The team was particularly encouraged by the enthusiasm surrounding the announced improvements for Aruna v3, which promise to further enhance the platform’s usability and performance.

The Aruna development team extends their gratitude to all participants for their valuable contributions and looks forward to continuing the collaboration with the NFDI4Microbiota community. Stay tuned for more updates as Aruna evolves to meet the growing demands of the scientific research community!
