## Aruna Development Team Presents at Salt Lake City Poster Session

[**📄 Accepting Mortality: Strategies for Ultra-Long Running Stateful Workloads in K8s**](https://kccncna2024.sched.com/event/1i7mo/poster-session-ps10-accepting-mortality-strategies-for-ultra-long-running-stateful-workloads-in-k8s-sebastian-beyvers-maria-hansen-giessen-university)

The Aruna development team recently embarked on an exciting journey to Salt Lake City to present at KubeCon's Poster Session track. Their presentation explored the challenges of running ultra-long stateful workloads, particularly when those workloads rely on in-memory processing and lack built-in persistence. This topic poses a unique challenge as one of Kubernetes’ fundamental principles is that Pods are mortal.

But, by leveraging Kubernetes and Argo Workflows, the team demonstrated that, despite the inherent challenges, it is possible to run ultra-long stateful workloads efficiently. Their approach incorporates strategic configuration, robust monitoring, and advanced techniques like CRIU (Checkpoint/Restore In Userspace) for seamless recovery from failures.

### A Warm Reception at KubeCon

The team’s presentation at KubeCon was met with great enthusiasm. Attendees engaged in thought-provoking discussions about best practices for stateful workloads, sharing their own experiences and insights. The session served as a valuable platform for exchanging knowledge, and the feedback received was overwhelmingly positive.
